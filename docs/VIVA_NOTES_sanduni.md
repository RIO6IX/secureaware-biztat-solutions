# Viva Notes Sanduni

Sanduni owns the Policy Management, Assignment and Acknowledgement module.

Key talking points:
- Policies have versions.
- Published versions must not be silently edited.
- Acknowledgement is recorded against the exact displayed version.
- Compliance is derived from assignments, acknowledgements and deadlines.

Likely questions:
1. Why version policies? To preserve historical evidence.
2. What is stored in acknowledgement evidence? User, policy version and timestamp.
3. What happens after a new version? Re-acknowledgement is required.
4. Who can assign policies? Authorized admin role.
5. Why restrict uploads? To reduce file upload attack risk.
