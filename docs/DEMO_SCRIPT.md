# Demo Script (whole system)

1. Security/HR Admin signs in. Show the audit log and role-based menus.
2. Policy module (Sanduni): create a new policy version, send it to IT and Management reviewers, approve and publish it, then compare versions. See [DEMO_SCRIPT_POLICY.md](DEMO_SCRIPT_POLICY.md).
3. Employee reads the policy to the end and acknowledges it, and gets a receipt with the SHA-256 of the text.
4. Training module (Chanuka): employee completes the assigned course, fails then passes the server-scored quiz, and opens the certificate. See [DEMO_SCRIPT_TRAINING.md](DEMO_SCRIPT_TRAINING.md).
5. Department Manager reviews team policy and training status for their own department and sends reminders.
6. Team explains password hashing, sessions, CSRF, RBAC, privacy, audit logging and backup.
