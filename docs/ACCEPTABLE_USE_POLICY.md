## Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of SecureAware and Biztat Solutions information assets for the academic prototype.

## Scope
This policy applies to users of SecureAware test accounts and to policy, training, acknowledgement, quiz and audit records created in the prototype.

## Responsibilities
Users must protect credentials, complete assigned acknowledgements and training, and report suspected misuse. Administrators must assign only appropriate work, avoid unnecessary personal data, and review audit evidence responsibly.

## Accounts Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be unique and protected. Production use should require MFA for administrator accounts.

## Acceptable Use
SecureAware may be used to read assigned policies, acknowledge displayed policy versions, complete assigned training, attempt quizzes and review authorized compliance records.

## Prohibited Use
Users must not share accounts, alter another user's records, upload executable files, bypass access controls, or enter confidential Biztat data without approval.

## Policy Acknowledgement and Training
Acknowledgements are recorded against the exact policy version displayed to the user. Training quiz results are calculated by the server.

## Data Handling and Privacy
The academic prototype uses dummy or anonymized accounts. Audit logs record actor, action, target and timestamp, but not passwords or session secrets.

## Incident Reporting
Suspected account misuse, incorrect access, or data exposure must be reported to the project team and academic supervisor.

## Alignment
This policy is aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.1, 5.10 and 6.3, NIST CSF 2.0 Protect Awareness and Training outcomes, and OWASP ASVS principles. This is an alignment claim, not certification.
